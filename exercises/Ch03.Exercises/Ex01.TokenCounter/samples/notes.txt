Tokens are the atomic unit of every LLM call. The model never sees your
characters or words directly; a tokenizer first splits the text into subword
pieces, and both your bill and your context window are measured in those
pieces. English prose averages roughly four characters per token, which makes
length divided by four a serviceable back-of-the-envelope estimate. Code and
JSON tokenize worse: symbols, brackets, and camelCase identifiers fragment
into more pieces per character, so the divisor shrinks. When a wrong estimate
would overflow the context window or under-bill a customer, switch to a real
BPE tokenizer — but for budgeting a folder of files, the heuristic is enough.
