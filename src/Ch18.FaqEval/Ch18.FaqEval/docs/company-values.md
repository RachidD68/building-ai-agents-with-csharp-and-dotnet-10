# Contoso Company Values

## Our Core Values

1. **Integrity First**: We do the right thing, even when no one is watching. Honesty and transparency are non-negotiable.

2. **Customer Obsession**: Our customers are at the heart of everything we do. We listen, respond quickly, and strive to exceed expectations.

3. **Innovation Without Fear**: We encourage bold thinking and calculated risks. Failure is a learning opportunity, not a reason for blame.

4. **One Team**: Collaboration across teams drives extraordinary results. We celebrate diverse perspectives and believe inclusion makes us stronger.

5. **Sustainability Matters**: We are committed to reducing our environmental footprint across all operations, from data centers to offices.

## Living Our Values

Every employee is expected to embody these values in their daily work. Our values are reflected in hiring decisions, performance reviews, and leadership development programs.
