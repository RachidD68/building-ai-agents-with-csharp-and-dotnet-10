# Contoso Code of Conduct

## Expected Behavior

Communicate respectfully in all channels. Give and receive feedback constructively. Respect confidential information and intellectual property. Report concerns through proper channels.

## Prohibited Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind is not tolerated. Misuse of company resources for personal gain is prohibited. Sharing confidential information with unauthorized parties will result in disciplinary action. Retaliation against anyone reporting concerns in good faith is strictly prohibited.

## Reporting Concerns

Report concerns through: direct conversation with your manager, the anonymous ethics hotline (1-800-555-ETHICS), or email to ethics@contoso.com. All reports are investigated promptly and handled confidentially.

## Consequences

Violations are handled on a case-by-case basis and may result in coaching, written warning, suspension, or termination depending on severity.
