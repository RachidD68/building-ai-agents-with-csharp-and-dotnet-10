# Contoso Employee Benefits

## Health Insurance

Contoso offers three medical plans: PPO, HMO, and a high-deductible plan with HSA. Dental and vision coverage are included at no additional cost. The company covers 90% of premiums for employees and 75% for dependents.

## Retirement

Contoso matches 401(k) contributions up to 6% of salary with immediate vesting. Free financial planning services are available through Fidelity.

## Parental Leave

Birth parents receive 16 weeks of fully paid leave. Non-birth parents receive 8 weeks. Adoption leave is 12 weeks. Leave must begin within 12 months of the qualifying event.

## Wellness Program

A $500 annual wellness stipend covers gym memberships, fitness equipment, or mental health services. Free counseling is available through the Employee Assistance Program.

## Learning and Development

Each employee has a $2,000 annual professional development budget for conferences, courses, certifications, and books. Tuition reimbursement up to $5,250 per year is also available.
