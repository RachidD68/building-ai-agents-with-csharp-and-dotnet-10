# Contoso Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Overview

Contoso reimburses employees for reasonable business expenses. Amounts over $100 require pre-approval from your manager.

## Submission Process

1. Collect all receipts.
2. Submit through the Contoso Expense Portal within 30 days.
3. Attach scanned or photographed receipts.
4. Manager approval is required within 5 business days.
5. Reimbursement is processed in the next payroll cycle.

## Meal Limits

During business travel, meal expenses are capped at $75 per day for domestic travel and $100 per day for international travel.

## Travel

Airfare, hotel, rental car, and ride-sharing are reimbursable. Business class is permitted for flights over 6 hours. First-class is not reimbursed.

## Professional Development

Conferences, training, and books are covered up to $2,000 per year with manager approval.

## Late Submissions

Expenses submitted more than 60 days after the transaction require VP-level approval and may not be reimbursed.
