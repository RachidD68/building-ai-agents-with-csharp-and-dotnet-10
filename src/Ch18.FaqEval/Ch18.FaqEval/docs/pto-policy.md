# Contoso PTO Policy

## Annual Allowance

Paid time off at Contoso is based on years of service:
- 0-2 years: 15 days per year
- 3-5 years: 20 days per year
- 6+ years: 25 days per year

PTO accrues monthly. Up to 5 unused days can carry over to the next year. Days beyond the carryover limit are forfeited on January 1.

## Requesting Time Off

Submit requests through the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance. For absences of 5+ consecutive days, submit at least 30 days ahead. Managers approve within 3 business days.

## Company Holidays

Contoso observes 11 company holidays per year, including New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and the day after, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, and New Year's Eve.
