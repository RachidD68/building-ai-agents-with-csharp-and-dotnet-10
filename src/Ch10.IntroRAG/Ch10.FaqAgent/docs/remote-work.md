# Contoso Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probationary period are eligible for remote work. Contractors and interns may request remote work with manager approval.

## Schedule

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. At least 2 days must be in-office for collaboration. Core hours are 10 AM to 3 PM in your local timezone.

## Home Office Equipment

Contoso provides a $500 stipend for home office setup including monitors, keyboards, and ergonomic equipment. IT supplies a company laptop and VPN credentials.

## Communication Tools

Slack is used for daily messaging. Microsoft Teams is for video meetings. Response times during core hours should not exceed 30 minutes.

## Security Requirements

Always use the company VPN when accessing internal systems. Personal devices must be enrolled in the MDM program before use for work tasks.
