# Contoso HR FAQ — Sample document for Hosted-Files RAG demo

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

New Contoso employees receive **15 days** of paid time off per year. The
allotment accrues monthly (1.25 days/month). Unused PTO carries over up to a
maximum of 30 days; any amount above 30 days at the end of the calendar year
is forfeited.

After **two years** of tenure, the annual allotment increases to **20 days**.
After **five years**, it increases to **25 days**.

## Holidays

Contoso observes 11 paid holidays per year, in addition to PTO. Each
business unit publishes its own holiday schedule.

## Sick Leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees accrue **8 hours of sick leave
per month** with no annual cap. Sick leave does not carry a maximum balance.

## Parental Leave

Birth parents receive **20 weeks** of paid parental leave. Non-birth parents
and adoptive parents receive **12 weeks**. Parental leave may be taken in
two blocks within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption.

## 401(k) Plan

Contoso matches employee 401(k) contributions dollar-for-dollar up to **6%**
of base salary. The match vests immediately. Employees may contribute up to
the IRS annual limit.

## Health Insurance

Contoso offers three medical plan options (Bronze, Silver, Gold) and a
high-deductible plan compatible with a Health Savings Account (HSA).
Coverage begins on the first day of the month following hire.

## Tuition Reimbursement

Contoso reimburses up to **$5,000 per year** in tuition for accredited
programs related to the employee's role. Reimbursement requires a passing
grade and continued employment for 12 months after the reimbursed term.
