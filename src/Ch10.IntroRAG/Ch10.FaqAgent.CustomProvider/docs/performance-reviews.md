# Contoso Performance Review Process

## Review Cycle

Performance reviews happen twice per year: a mid-year check-in in June and a full annual review in December. Both involve self-assessment, peer feedback, and manager evaluation.

## Goal Setting

At the start of each year, employees set 3-5 SMART goals with their manager. Goals should align with team objectives and company priorities.

## Peer Feedback

Employees nominate 3-5 peers for feedback. Reviews focus on collaboration, communication, and technical contributions. All feedback is shared with the employee.

## Rating Scale

Performance uses a 5-point scale: Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations Plus, Meets Expectations, Needs Improvement, and Unsatisfactory. Ratings influence compensation decisions.

## Development Plans

Every employee creates a development plan identifying skills to build, training to pursue, and career goals. Managers actively support these plans through regular 1:1 meetings.
