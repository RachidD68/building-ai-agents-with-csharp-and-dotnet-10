# Contoso Security Policy

## Data Classification

All company data is classified into four tiers: Public, Internal, Confidential, and Restricted. Employees must handle data according to its classification level.

## Access Control

Access to systems and data follows the principle of least privilege. Access reviews are conducted quarterly. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for all internal systems.

## Device Security

Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled. Automatic updates must be enabled for operating systems and applications. Personal devices require MDM enrollment before accessing company resources.

## Incident Reporting

Report security incidents immediately to security@contoso.com or call the security hotline at extension 9911. Do not attempt to investigate incidents independently.

## Password Policy

Passwords must be at least 16 characters with a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols. Password managers are encouraged. Passwords must not be reused across systems.
