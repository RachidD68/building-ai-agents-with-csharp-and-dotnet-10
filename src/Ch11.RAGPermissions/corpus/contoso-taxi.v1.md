title: Taxi rides (Contoso)
---
Contoso reimburses a taxi ride on a business trip only when no public transport runs for that journey. Keep the receipt and attach it to the claim.

Any claim above the low-value limit of 50.00 EUR waits for a finance reviewer, who must not be the employee who filed it.
