title: Taxi rides (Contoso)
---
From 16 November 2026, Contoso reimburses a taxi or ride-hailing trip on business travel that starts or ends between 22:00 and 06:00, whether or not public transport runs. Keep the receipt and attach it to the claim.

Any claim above the low-value limit of 50.00 EUR waits for a finance reviewer, who must not be the employee who filed it.
