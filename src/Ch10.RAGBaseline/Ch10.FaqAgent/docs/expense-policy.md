# Contoso Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Overview

Contoso reimburses employees for reasonable business expenses, in EUR. A claim of 50.00 EUR or less is approved automatically; a claim above 50.00 EUR waits for a finance reviewer, who cannot be the employee who filed it.

## Submission Process

1. Collect all receipts.
2. Submit through the Contoso Expense Portal within 30 days.
3. Attach scanned or photographed receipts.
4. Reimbursement is processed in the next payroll cycle.

## Meal Limits

During business travel, meal expenses are capped at 75.00 EUR per day for domestic travel and 100.00 EUR per day for international travel.

## Travel

Airfare, hotel, rental car, and ride-sharing are reimbursable. Business class is permitted for flights over 6 hours. First-class is not reimbursed.

## Professional Development

Conferences, training, and books are covered up to 2000.00 EUR per year; your manager approves the spend before you book.

## Late Submissions

Expenses submitted more than 60 days after the transaction require VP-level approval and may not be reimbursed.
