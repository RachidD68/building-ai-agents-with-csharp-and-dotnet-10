# Contoso Leave and PTO Policy

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

Contoso offers a flexible PTO policy based on tenure:

| Tenure | Annual PTO Days |
|--------|----------------|
| 0-2 years | 15 days |
| 3-5 years | 20 days |
| 6+ years | 25 days |

PTO accrues monthly and can be carried over up to 5 days into the next calendar year. Unused PTO beyond the carryover limit is forfeited on January 1.

## Requesting Time Off

1. Submit a PTO request in the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance.
2. Your manager must approve the request within 3 business days.
3. For requests of 5+ consecutive days, submit at least 30 days in advance.
4. Holiday blackout periods may apply during critical project phases.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. Sick leave does not carry over. A doctor's note is required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days.

## Parental Leave

- Birth parents: 16 weeks paid leave.
- Non-birth parents: 8 weeks paid leave.
- Adoption: 12 weeks paid leave.
- Leave must begin within 12 months of the qualifying event.

## Bereavement Leave

- Immediate family: 5 paid days.
- Extended family: 3 paid days.

## Jury Duty

Contoso provides full pay for the duration of jury duty service.
