# Contoso New Employee Onboarding Guide

## Welcome to Contoso

Congratulations on joining Contoso! This guide walks you through your first two weeks.

## Week 1: Getting Started

### Day 1
- Pick up your laptop and badge from IT (Building A, Room 102).
- Complete HR paperwork in the onboarding portal.
- Meet your manager and team for a welcome lunch.
- Set up your email, Slack, and Teams accounts.

### Days 2-3
- Complete mandatory compliance training (approximately 4 hours).
- Review the employee handbook on the Contoso intranet.
- Set up your development environment with help from your buddy.
- Attend the "Contoso Culture" session with the People team.

### Days 4-5
- Shadow a team member on a current project.
- Attend your first team standup meeting.
- Complete security awareness training.
- Schedule 1:1 meetings with key stakeholders.

## Week 2: Ramping Up

- Begin contributing to a starter project assigned by your manager.
- Complete the "Contoso Engineering Practices" workshop.
- Set up your home office equipment using the $500 stipend.
- Review and acknowledge all company policies in the HR portal.

## Probationary Period

The probationary period is 90 days. During this time, you will have bi-weekly check-ins with your manager. At the end of 90 days, a formal review will determine your transition to regular employee status.
