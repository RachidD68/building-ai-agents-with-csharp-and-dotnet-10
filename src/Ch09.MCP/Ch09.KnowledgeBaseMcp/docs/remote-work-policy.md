# Contoso Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

All full-time employees who have completed their probationary period (90 days) are eligible for remote work. Contractors and interns may request remote work on a case-by-case basis with manager approval.

## Schedule

- Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
- At least 2 days per week must be spent in the office for collaboration.
- Core hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in the employee's local timezone.
- Flexible start and end times are permitted outside core hours.

## Equipment

Contoso provides a $500 stipend for home office setup, covering items such as monitors, keyboards, and ergonomic chairs. IT will supply a company laptop and VPN credentials.

## Communication

- Slack is the primary communication tool for day-to-day messaging.
- Microsoft Teams is used for video meetings and formal presentations.
- Response times during core hours should not exceed 30 minutes.

## Security

Employees must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems. Personal devices are not permitted for work tasks unless enrolled in the MDM program.
