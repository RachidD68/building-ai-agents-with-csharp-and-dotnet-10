# Contoso Code of Conduct

## Purpose

Contoso is committed to maintaining a professional, respectful, and inclusive workplace. This code of conduct applies to all employees, contractors, and visitors.

## Core Values

1. **Integrity**: We act honestly and transparently in all business dealings.
2. **Respect**: We treat every individual with dignity regardless of background.
3. **Innovation**: We encourage creative thinking and constructive feedback.
4. **Accountability**: We own our work and its outcomes.
5. **Collaboration**: We believe the best results come from working together.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate respectfully in all channels (in-person, email, chat, meetings).
- Give and receive feedback constructively.
- Respect confidential information and intellectual property.
- Report any concerns through the ethics hotline or to your manager.

## Prohibited Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind.
- Misuse of company resources for personal gain.
- Sharing confidential information with unauthorized parties.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Reporting Concerns

Employees can report concerns through:
- Direct conversation with their manager.
- The anonymous ethics hotline: 1-800-555-ETHICS.
- Email: ethics@contoso.com.

All reports are investigated promptly and handled confidentially.
