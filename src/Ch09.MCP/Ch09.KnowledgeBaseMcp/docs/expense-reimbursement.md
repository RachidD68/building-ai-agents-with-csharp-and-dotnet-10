# Contoso Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Overview

Contoso reimburses employees for reasonable business expenses incurred while performing job duties. All expenses must be pre-approved by a manager for amounts exceeding $100.

## Submission Process

1. Collect all receipts (digital or paper).
2. Submit expenses through the Contoso Expense Portal within 30 days.
3. Attach scanned receipts or photos of receipts.
4. Manager approval is required within 5 business days.
5. Reimbursement is processed in the next payroll cycle after approval.

## Eligible Expenses

- Travel: airfare, hotel, rental car, ride-sharing services
- Meals: up to $75/day for domestic travel, $100/day for international
- Office supplies: items under $50 do not require pre-approval
- Professional development: conferences, training, books (up to $2,000/year)
- Client entertainment: meals and events with prior manager approval

## Non-Eligible Expenses

- Personal entertainment or leisure activities
- Alcohol (unless part of an approved client event)
- First-class airfare (business class for flights over 6 hours is permitted)
- Spouse or family travel expenses

## Late Submissions

Expenses submitted more than 60 days after the transaction date will not be reimbursed without VP-level approval.
