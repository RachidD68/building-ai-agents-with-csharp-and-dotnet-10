# Contoso spend-policy FAQ

## Per-trip limits
- Domestic travel: USD 150 / day for meals; USD 250 / night for lodging.
- International travel: USD 90 / day for meals; USD 200 / night for lodging.
- Receipts are required for every line item above USD 25.

## Allowed categories
- Meals (excluding alcohol).
- Lodging (single occupancy; reasonable in-room amenities).
- Ground transport (taxi, ride-share, public transit).
- Air travel (economy by default; premium-economy for flights over 6 hours).
- Conference fees (pre-approved by line manager).

## Requires approval
- Any single line item above USD 500.
- Alcohol — only at customer-facing dinners; manager sign-off required.
- Equipment purchases over USD 200.
- International travel for non-customer-facing roles.

## Not allowed
- Personal entertainment (movies, mini-bar).
- First-class air travel.
- Cash advances.
- Gifts to customers above USD 50.

## Submission deadline
Expense reports must be filed within 30 days of the trip's end. Reports
filed after 60 days require Finance Director approval.
