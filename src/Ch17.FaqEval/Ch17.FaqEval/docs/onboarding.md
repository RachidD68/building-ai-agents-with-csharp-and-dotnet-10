# Contoso New Employee Onboarding

## Week 1

Day 1: Pick up laptop and badge from IT (Building A, Room 102). Complete HR paperwork. Meet your team for welcome lunch. Set up email, Slack, and Teams.

Days 2-3: Complete mandatory compliance training (4 hours). Review the employee handbook. Set up your development environment with your onboarding buddy.

Days 4-5: Shadow a team member. Attend your first standup. Complete security awareness training. Schedule 1:1 meetings with stakeholders.

## Week 2

Begin contributing to a starter project. Complete the Engineering Practices workshop. Set up home office using the $500 stipend. Acknowledge all company policies in the HR portal.

## Probationary Period

The probation period is 90 days with bi-weekly manager check-ins. A formal review at 90 days determines transition to regular status.
