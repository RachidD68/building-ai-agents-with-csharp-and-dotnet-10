# Contoso Sick Leave Policy

## Annual Allowance

Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. Sick leave does not carry over to the next year and resets on January 1.

## Usage

Sick leave may be used for personal illness, medical appointments, or caring for an immediate family member who is ill.

## Notification

Notify your manager as early as possible on the day of absence. For planned medical appointments, provide at least 48 hours notice.

## Extended Illness

A doctor's note is required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days. For extended medical leave beyond 10 days, employees may apply for short-term disability through our insurance provider.

## Mental Health

Sick leave may also be used for mental health days. Contoso encourages employees to prioritize their mental well-being and offers free counseling through the Employee Assistance Program.
